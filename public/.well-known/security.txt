# Responsible Disclosure Policy

We take security seriously. If you discover a vulnerability, please report
it to security@turfzo.com with the subject "Security Disclosure".

We will:
- Acknowledge your report within 48 hours
- Provide a triage assessment within 5 business days
- Keep you informed of remediation progress

We request that you:
- Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond demonstrating it
- Do not access or modify user data without permission
- Do not disclose the issue publicly until we have remediated it

We commit to not pursuing legal action against security researchers who
follow this policy in good faith.
